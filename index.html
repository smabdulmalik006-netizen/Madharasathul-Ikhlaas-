📜 Tajweed Sattam - Full Rules

1. Makhraj

Ovvoru ezhuthum thondai, naakku, udhadu la irundhu varum.

2. Noon Saakinah - 4 Sattam

Izhar: Thelivaga - ء ه ع ح غ خ
Idgham: Serthu - ي ر م ل و ن
Ikhfa: Maraithu - 15 ezhuthu
Iqlab: Ba vandha Noon ah Meem ah maathanum

3. Meem Saakinah - 3 Sattam

Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi, Izhar Shafawi

4. Ghunnah

Noon, Meem ku Shaddah irundha 2 alavu mookula osai.

5. Mad

Tabee: 2 alavu, Muttasil: 4-5 alavu, Lazim: 6 alavu

6. Qalqalah

5 ezhuthu: ق ط ب ج د - Qutb Jad - Edhir olikkum

7. Tafkheem & Tarqeeq

Ganam: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ, Melidu: Vera ellam

8. Waqf

م: Niruthanum, ج: Niruthalam, لا: Nirutha koodadhu
